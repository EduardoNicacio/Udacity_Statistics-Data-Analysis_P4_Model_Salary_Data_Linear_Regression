# Project Overview

## Project Scenario

In this project, you will build a linear model and interpret the results of the model. Specifically, you will look at what variables are related to salaries.

## Workspace

You are provided with a Jupyter workspace in this project. You will see the workspace on the "Workspace" page. In the workspace, you will find the project's Jupyter Notebook and the dataset.

You are recommended to use the workspace to complete the project, but if you want to work locally, you can find all the needed files in the **Supporting Materials** section later on this page.

## Starter Files

Your goal is to work through the provided **Jupyter Notebook** to understand the features that are related to salary. You will also be provided with a **CSV file** containing the dataset. You will use the data and the Jupyter Notebook to complete the analysis.

Once you have completed the notebook, you will present your results using a **presentation**. There are many placeholders in the template that must be replaced with results you obtain from the analysis. **You must submit both the notebook and the presentation for this project**.
